## A. Business core
1. Sells (one sentence):
   Wood-fire cooking on the Wörthersee shore — dry-aged Carinthian beef, lake fish, and one Kasnudeln dish done properly. Guests pay for the room, the fire, and the view.

2. Best customer:
   Couples from Graz or Vienna who drive down for a long weekend at the lake. They know Steirereck exists but want something quieter — not a hotel dining room, not a Stadt gastro pub. Second table: Carinthia food tourists who heard about the Reinanke and will book three weeks out.

3. Why them over the alternative:
   Every other lakeside place either has the view without a real kitchen, or the kitchen without the view. We have both in one room — open fire, floor-to-ceiling glass to the Lendkanal, and a menu that stops at twelve dishes. My grandfather ran a Gasthaus in Villach; I kept the fire, lost the schnitzel wallpaper.

4. Known for in 3 years:
   The place on the Wörthersee where the flame is the menu. People say "Seeblick" the way they say the lake name.
