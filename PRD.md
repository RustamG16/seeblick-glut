# PRD — SEEBLICK & GLUT (Fensterplatz redesign)

## Problem and goal
The current site is a black, full-bleed, scroll-scrubbed page with a 130 MB frame payload. The owner wants a complete change: a calm, award-level page that gets Friday and Saturday dinner booked by 19:00 without phone calls during service.

## Audience
Primary: couples from Graz/Vienna on a Wörthersee weekend. Secondary: Carinthian food tourists booking three weeks out.

## Success criteria
- Reservation (phone/e-mail) reachable from header, Praktisches and the closing band.
- The four FAQ answers (Mondays, parking, terrace, gluten-free) visible without interaction.
- Every video framed, ≤720 lines; no watermark visible; page complete with posters only.
- Finish review PASS against `DESIGN.md`; performance targets in `.olympus/06-build-plan.md` measured.

## Data and publication boundary
Static site, no cookies, no analytics in scope. Legal pages keep their text; the UID placeholder must be replaced by the client before launch.

## Out of scope
Booking engine, multi-language, CMS, full menu, newsletter, Instagram feed.
